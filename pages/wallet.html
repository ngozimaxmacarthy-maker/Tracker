One conflict worth knowing about

Groceries to Bilt, dining to the Sapphire. Your category choice already settled it.

Bilt's 3× is one category, not two — groceries or dining, chosen once and locked for the calendar year. Yours is set to groceries, so dining on the Bilt earns the base 1×. That single setting decides the whole split.

It works in your favour. Groceries are the steadier monthly number, so they are the better engine for the $899 of non-housing spend that keeps your HOA earning 1.25× — and they earn 3× on the way. Dining then goes to the Sapphire Preferred at 3×, worth the same per dollar, with nothing lost. Gas, streaming, vacation rentals and Chase Travel stay on the Sapphire too; Bilt does not cover them.

Had the category been left on its default of dining, these two goals would have been fighting over the same transactions all year.
